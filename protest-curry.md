This recipe is the result of me refusing to follow the recipe for a type of curry I wanted to make because I thought it was wrong and hated the website (hence this website). 

## Ingredients
- __red__ lentils
- (basmati) rice
- coconut milk
- vegetable stock cube
- garlic
- tomato paste (which is obviously __red__)
- __red__ (hot) chili peppers
- __red__ (hot) chili pepper powder
- cumin powder
- turmeric powder
- ginger powder (fresh ginger is also ok, of course)
- garam masala
- lemon/lime
- __red__ pepper
- coriander
- oil
- salt

### Optional
- __red__ peppers (or any other greens that are actually __red__)
- tomato puree (which is also obviously __red__)

## Equipment
- wok

## Steps
1. - chop the garlic finely
   - slice the red hot chili pepper(s)
   - dice the peppers or any other vegetables you want to use
   - warm up some oil in the wok
2. - sauté the garlic and chili together with some tomato paste
   - rinse the lentils
   - prepare the vegetable broth (if you are planning to add tomato puree, use very little water)
3. add the vegetables and let them cook for a few minutes
4. pour the coconut milk into the wok
5. - add all the powders
   - stir
6. - add the lentils and let cook on medium-low heat, stirring every now and then. As the mixture dries up, irrigate with broth and, optionally, tomato puree
   - 1. - rinse the rice
        - pour some water (2 units per unit of rice) into a separate pot and bring it to a boil
     2. salt the water and add the rice. Let it cook on low heat until all water has been absorbed, without stirring
7. when the lentils have become soft, turn off the heat
8. add abundant lemon/lime juice and stir
9. season with red pepper and coriander
